How pricing works

Staff numbers

Cost is based on how many staff need the app on their phone, not a flat rate per property.

Site size and layout

A single boutique hotel and a multi-building aged care facility aren't priced the same way.

One quote, before you commit

We walk through your shifts and layout on a call, then send a written quote. No pricing is published without that conversation.
