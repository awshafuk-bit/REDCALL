Built for teams working alone or spread thin

Redcall is designed for properties where staff numbers are small relative to the space they cover, and where a single person is often the only one aware something has gone wrong.

Boutique hotels

Night shifts

A single night auditor or receptionist is often the only staff member on site, with no one nearby to call on if something happens.

Front-desk incidents

An intoxicated or aggressive guest, or a medical event in the lobby, needs other staff aware fast, wherever they are on the property.

Staff spread across floors

Housekeeping and maintenance staff working alone in rooms or back-of-house areas have no easy way to reach the desk quickly.

Aged care facilities

Lone workers

Support workers are frequently alone with a resident, particularly overnight, with colleagues on other wings or floors.

Medical incidents

Falls and sudden health events need the rest of the on-duty team aware immediately, not after someone finds a phone and dials around.

Facility-wide awareness

Incidents involving a resident, such as a wandering event, benefit from every on-shift staff member knowing at once, not just the closest one.

Small venues

Small teams, large spaces

A handful of staff covering a bar, function room, or event space can't always see or hear each other across the venue.

Front-of-house incidents

Conflicts between patrons or a medical event on the floor need security and management aware straight away.

Closing alone

The last staff member closing up is often working without anyone else on the property to notice if something's wrong.
