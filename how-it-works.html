How RedCall works

RedCall is built for the moments a workplace can't afford confusion. One alert starts a response that your team can see.

1. One alert reaches the team

No hunting for the right person, no relayed messages. Everyone on the team receives the alert at once, and people recorded as on site get the loud one.

2. Staff see what to do

Each person sees what has been raised and what is happening, and can reply to say whether they are safe or not safe.

3. Managers coordinate in real time

Managers can see who has replied safe or not safe, and who hasn't responded yet. Silence isn't treated as safety. They can send messages to the team while the incident is still unfolding.

4. Every incident is recorded automatically

What happened, who responded and when is recorded while the incident unfolds, so your workplace has its own record afterwards.

Before you rely on it

In an emergency, call 000 first. RedCall helps your team find out and respond. It does not contact the emergency services and it does not replace them. RedCall is currently in a pilot, and some alert features are still awaiting approval. See Support for how alerts work today.
