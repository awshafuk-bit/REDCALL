Security and data handling

This page is a general summary. Our Privacy Policy is the full description of how we collect, use and protect information.

Where data is held

Our database is hosted in Singapore. Some of the service providers we use to send notifications, text messages and emails operate in the United States and other countries.

Who can access it

Management tools, including incident reports and team details, are limited to a workplace's authorised managers (Admins). Team members who are part of an alert can see the details of that alert. A limited number of RedCall personnel can access information where needed to provide support, keep the service secure and meet legal obligations.

Manager sign-in

Managers sign in with their email address, then a one-time code sent to that address, followed by a code from an authenticator app.

Incident records

Incident records are kept for the workplace's own records. We email a report to the address the workplace provides when a real incident or a drill ends. Workplaces should save their own copies, as RedCall is not an archive.

Encryption and contact

Information is sent over encrypted connections. No system is completely secure. For security or privacy questions, email info@redcall.com.au.
