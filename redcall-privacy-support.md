# RedCall Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

RedCall is a staff safety alert app for businesses. It is run by Valmere Group Pty Ltd
(ABN 13 702 167 780, ACN 702 167 780), trading as RedCall ("we", "us"). This policy explains
what we collect, why, who sees it, and how you can delete it. We follow the Australian
Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

Contact: info@redcall.com.au

### What RedCall does

When someone at a workplace raises an emergency, RedCall alerts the rest of the team, shows
who is safe, and keeps a record of the incident for the workplace.

### What we collect

**About you.** Your name, mobile number, preferred language, the workplace you belong to and
your role (staff, manager or owner). Managers and owners also have an email address.

**During an alert.** The alert itself: the type of threat, the time, who started it and who
ended it, an optional place description chosen in the app (for example "Lobby"), your
"safe" or "not safe" reply, and messages sent in the alert chat.

**On-site status.** If you allow location, your phone checks whether you are inside or
outside your workplace's area. That check happens on your phone. We receive only the answer
"inside" or "outside", plus the time. We do not receive or store your coordinates, and we do
not keep a history of where you have been. Your manager cannot see where you are.

**To reach you.** A device token so we can send notifications to your phone, and your mobile
number so we can send login codes and, in some alerts, a text message.

**Technical.** Basic logs that help us keep the service secure and fix faults.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, microphone or advertising identifiers. We do not
show ads and we do not sell your information.

### Why we use it

To sign you in, deliver alerts, show who is safe, keep an incident record for your workplace,
decide who should hear a loud alert, keep the service secure, and answer your questions.

### Who can see it

- People at your workplace who are in an alert can see its details, the names of the team
  and who has marked safe.
- Managers and owners at your workplace can see the team list and incident reports.
- Nobody at your workplace can see your location.
- We do not share your information with anyone else, except the providers below, or where the
  law requires it.

### Providers that handle data for us

- **Supabase**: database and sign-in. Data is stored in Singapore.
- **Google (Firebase) and Apple**: delivery of push notifications.
- **Twilio**: login codes and alert text messages. Twilio is based in the United States.

Because of these providers, your information can be handled outside Australia, in Singapore
and the United States. We use providers that protect data to a standard comparable to ours.
Information travels over encrypted connections.

### Keeping and deleting your information

You can delete your account in the app: Profile, then Delete account. You have 30 days to
change your mind by signing in again. After 30 days your personal profile is removed.
Incident reports your workplace already holds stay in its records, and they show you as a
"former staff member". [CONFIRM: how long a workplace keeps incident reports, and whether
you want to state a fixed period.]

### Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information or ask us to correct it by emailing
info@redcall.com.au. We will reply within 30 days. If you are not happy with how we handled
your information, tell us first. You can also contact the Office of the Australian
Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

### Children

RedCall is for working adults. It is not designed for people under 16 [CONFIRM], and we do
not knowingly collect information from them.

### Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date at the top.

# RedCall Support

Email: info@redcall.com.au [CONFIRM: reply time you will commit to, for example "we reply
within 2 business days"]

**In an emergency, call 000 first.** RedCall helps your team find out and respond. It does
not replace the emergency services.

### Common questions

**I can't sign in.**
Staff sign in with their mobile number and a code sent by text. Use the number your manager
added. An Australian number can be typed as 0400 000 000. A number from another country needs
its country code, for example +44 7700 900123. Managers sign in with email, password and an
authenticator code. If it still fails, ask your manager to check that your number is on the
team list and that your account isn't frozen.

**I'm not getting alerts.**
Check that notifications are on for RedCall in your phone's Settings, and that Do Not Disturb
or Focus is not blocking them. Keep the app signed in. If you turned notifications off during
setup, open Settings, then RedCall, then Notifications to turn them back on.

**Why does RedCall ask for my location?**
So a loud alert rings the people who are actually at the workplace, and not the whole team on
their day off. Your phone only checks inside or outside. We don't store where you are and your
manager can't see it. Choose "Always" for it to work when the app is closed. If you say no,
you will still get alerts, but they arrive quietly unless your manager has put you on the
always-loud list.

**Why didn't my phone ring?**
A loud alert rings for people who are on site or on the always-loud list. Silent threat types
never ring, so a sound doesn't give away where someone is hiding. Everyone still receives the
alert and can open it.

**How do I delete my account?**
In the app, open Profile, then Delete account. You have 30 days to change your mind.

**I raised an alert by mistake.**
Press "Pressed by mistake? Cancel alert" within 2 minutes. After that, use your workplace's
cancel code or ask a manager.

**I'm a business and want to use RedCall.**
Email info@redcall.com.au and we will set you up.

### Report a problem

Email info@redcall.com.au with your phone model, the app version (Profile, About RedCall)
and what happened. Please don't include passwords or login codes.
