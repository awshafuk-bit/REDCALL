Common questions

I can't sign in.

Staff sign in with their mobile number and a code sent by text. Use the number your manager added. An Australian number can be typed as 0400 000 000. A number from another country needs its country code, for example +44 7700 900123. Managers sign in with email, password and an authenticator code. If it still fails, ask your manager to check that your number is on the team list and that your account isn't frozen.

I'm not getting alerts.

Check that notifications are on for RedCall in your phone's Settings, and that Do Not Disturb or Focus is not blocking them. Keep the app signed in. If you turned notifications off during setup, open Settings, then RedCall, then Notifications to turn them back on.

Why does RedCall ask for my location?

So a loud alert rings the people who are actually at the workplace, and not the whole team on their day off. Your phone only checks inside or outside. We don't store where you are and your manager can't see it. Choose "Always" for it to work when the app is closed. If you say no, you will still get alerts, but they arrive quietly unless your manager has put you on the always-loud list.

Why didn't my phone ring?

A loud alert rings for people who are on site or on the always-loud list. Silent threat types never ring, so a sound doesn't give away where someone is hiding. Everyone still receives the alert and can open it.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile, then Delete account. You have 30 days to change your mind.

I raised an alert by mistake.

Press "Pressed by mistake? Cancel alert" within 2 minutes. After that, use your workplace's cancel code or ask a manager.

I'm a business and want to use RedCall.

Email info@redcall.com.au and we will set you up.

Report a problem

Email info@redcall.com.au with your phone model, the app version (Profile, About RedCall) and what happened. Please don't include passwords or login codes.
