What Redcall is

Redcall is an awareness and coordination tool for on-site staff. It helps staff raise an alert, follow guidance for a situation, and confirm who is safe, and gives a manager a way to coordinate that response.

Redcall is not a personal panic button, a tactical responder, or an emergency service. It does not replace calling 000, and emergency response remains the responsibility of your staff and emergency services at all times.
