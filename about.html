About Us

Most safety and security products are designed to solve a broad range of problems across a broad range of industries.

RedCall was built differently.

From the beginning, our focus has been hotels and one specific challenge: what happens when an incident requires an entire team to respond, not just one person.

When communication is fragmented, people work from different information. Updates get missed. Assumptions get made. Managers are left trying to piece together a picture of what's happening while the situation is still unfolding.

We continue to focus on hotels because we believe specialised systems outperform generic ones. The better we understand the environment we're building for, the better we can design for the people working in it.

RedCall is owned and operated by Valmere Group Pty Ltd, an Australian company. We're focused on understanding the safety, security and operational challenges hotels face, and turning those insights into practical solutions that work in the real world, not just in a product demo.
