About Redcall

Boutique hotels and aged care facilities are often staffed by small teams covering a lot of ground, especially overnight. The usual options are enterprise security systems built for large sites, or informal habits like phone trees and calling out down a hallway.

Redcall was built to sit between those two: software staff already carry with them, doing one job well, getting the right people aware of an incident fast and giving a manager a clear way to coordinate the response.
