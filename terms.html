4. Use of alerts

4.1 Alerts are for genuine emergencies and for approved practice drills only. You must not raise an alert to test the Service, as a joke, to attract attention or for any purpose other than a real emergency or a drill.

4.2 A drill must be run only by an Admin, using the drill function. A drill is clearly labelled as a test, is recorded as a drill, and does not sound an alarm.

4.3 A person who raises an alert by mistake may cancel it from the alert screen within two minutes. After that, the alert may be ended only with the Workplace's cancel code or by an Admin. Everyone is told when an alert was a false alarm.

4.4 Only one alert may be active for a Workplace at a time.

4.5 Some threat types are silent. For a silent alert RedCall does not play an alarm sound on any phone, so that RedCall itself does not reveal where a person is hiding. When an alert is silent, or when no threat type is chosen within 15 seconds, we also send a text message to the Workplace's team so that the alert reaches them by a second route. A text message is delivered by your mobile network and may make the sound your phone uses for messages, according to your phone's settings. A Workplace is responsible for deciding, and telling its team, how phones should be set during a silent threat.

4.6 A loud alert sounds on the phones of people whose phones have reported within the last 24 hours that they are at the Workplace, and on the phones of people the Workplace has placed on its always loud list. Everyone else in the team receives the alert as a notification without the loud sound and can open it and reply.

4.7 Where Apple permits it and you have allowed it in your iPhone settings, the opening alert of an incident that has an audible threat type may be sent as an iPhone Critical Alert to people who are on site or on the Workplace's always loud list. A Critical Alert can sound even when your phone is on silent or Do Not Disturb. Silent threat types do not use Critical Alerts. Reminders that follow, and chat notifications, are ordinary notifications. Critical Alerts are not used for drills, for broadcast messages or for any marketing or routine purpose. You can change this setting for RedCall at any time in your iPhone settings.

4.8 Admins must use the broadcast function only for messages that concern the safety or operation of the Workplace's team. Broadcasts and chat messages must not be used for advertising or any unrelated purpose.

4.9 To limit false alarms, RedCall limits how many loud alerts one person, and one Workplace, can raise in an hour. Above the limit an alert is still delivered to the team, but without the loud sound, and the person who raised it is told on their screen. The most serious threat types are not limited in this way. A Workplace should call its team directly if it is told that an alert went out without sound.
