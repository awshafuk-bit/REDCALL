Workplace Emergency Alerts & Response Coordination

RedCall helps workplaces alert staff during emergencies, track who is safe or needs assistance, and coordinate responses in real time.

Built for the moments a workplace can't afford confusion.

See RedCall in your workplace

You already know what an incident looks like at your workplace when things aren't clear. The confusion, the delay, not knowing where everyone is. A live demo shows you what it looks like when everyone knows what's happening.

In one session, we walk you through the entire response, from the first alert to the final all-clear. You'll see what staff see. You'll see what management sees. And you'll see the moment those two views connect, usually the exact point where things fall apart without a system like this.

We'll also look at your workplace specifically: the layout, the shift patterns, and the places where messages get lost or people get missed. Every workplace has them. Most managers already know where theirs are.

If RedCall isn't right for your operation, we'll say so directly. We're not trying to fill a sales quota. We're trying to fix a real gap, and that only works if it's the right fit.

What we'll cover

  • How one alert reaches your whole team, instantly
  • How staff know what to do, without confusion
  • How management coordinates the response in real time
  • How incidents are recorded automatically

How RedCall works

Emergency. Everyone knows.

No hunting for the right person, no relayed messages. The whole team is alerted the second it matters.

No one falls through the cracks.

Silence isn't treated as safety. If someone doesn't check in, you'll know instead of finding out later.

Every incident, documented.

What happened, who responded, and how long it took. Recorded automatically while the incident unfolds.
