How Redcall works

Step one

Trigger

A staff member opens the app on their own phone, taps the alert button, and selects what is happening from a short list of situations.

Step two

Everyone responds

On-site staff are notified immediately, see guidance written for that specific situation, and mark themselves safe or not safe.

Step three

Manager coordinates

A manager sees who has responded, follows up directly with anyone who has not, and closes the alert once the situation is resolved.
