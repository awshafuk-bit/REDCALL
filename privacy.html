RedCall Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

RedCall is a staff safety alert app for businesses. It is run by Valmere Group Pty Ltd (ABN 13 702 167 780, ACN 702 167 780), trading as RedCall ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and how you can delete it. We follow the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

Contact: info@redcall.com.au

What RedCall does

When someone at a workplace raises an emergency, RedCall alerts the rest of the team, shows who is safe, and keeps a record of the incident for the workplace.

What we collect

About you. Your name, mobile number, preferred language, the workplace you belong to and your role (staff, manager or owner). Managers and owners also have an email address.

During an alert. The alert itself: the type of threat, the time, who started it and who ended it, an optional place description chosen in the app (for example "Lobby"), your "safe" or "not safe" reply, and messages sent in the alert chat.

On-site status. If you allow location, your phone checks whether you are inside or outside your workplace's area. That check happens on your phone. We receive only the answer "inside" or "outside", plus the time. We do not receive or store your coordinates, and we do not keep a history of where you have been. Your manager cannot see where you are.

To reach you. A device token so we can send notifications to your phone, and your mobile number so we can send login codes and, in some alerts, a text message.

Technical. Basic logs that help us keep the service secure and fix faults.

Website visitors. If you request a demo on redcall.com.au, we collect your name, email address and hotel name. We use them only to reply to your request. We don't use this website to track you or show you ads.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, microphone or advertising identifiers. We do not show ads and we do not sell your information.

Why we use it

To sign you in, deliver alerts, show who is safe, keep an incident record for your workplace, decide who should hear a loud alert, keep the service secure, and answer your questions.

Who can see it

  • People at your workplace who are in an alert can see its details, the names of the team and who has marked safe.
  • Managers and owners at your workplace can see the team list and incident reports.
  • Nobody at your workplace can see your location.
  • We do not share your information with anyone else, except the providers below, or where the law requires it.

Providers that handle data for us

  • Supabase: database and sign-in. Data is stored in Singapore.
  • Google (Firebase) and Apple: delivery of push notifications.
  • Twilio: login codes and alert text messages. Twilio is based in the United States.

Because of these providers, your information can be handled outside Australia, in Singapore and the United States. We use providers that protect data to a standard comparable to ours. Information travels over encrypted connections.

Keeping and deleting your information

You can delete your account in the app: Profile, then Delete account. You have 30 days to change your mind by signing in again. After 30 days your personal profile is removed. Incident reports your workplace already holds stay in its records, and they show you as a "former staff member". Incident reports stay in your workplace's records for as long as the workplace needs them for its safety records.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information or ask us to correct it by emailing info@redcall.com.au. We will reply within 30 days. If you are not happy with how we handled your information, tell us first. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Children

RedCall is for working adults. It is not designed for people under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date at the top.
